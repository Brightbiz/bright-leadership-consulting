## Noted caveats

- The ICO's storage-and-access (cookies) guidance is under review following the
  Data (Use and Access) Act 2025; wording may shift.
- No discrete CMA publication on CPD-accreditation claims in executive education
  was located; the CAP Code and the Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading
  Regulations 2008 are the operative sources.
