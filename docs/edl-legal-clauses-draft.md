## 1. Retention periods (UK GDPR Article 13(2)(a) — ICO requires a period or the criteria)

Proposed wording for the programme privacy notice:

> **How long we keep your information**
>
> | What we hold | How long we keep it | Why |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | Applications that are declined, withdrawn or waitlisted | 12 months from the date of the decision | So we can tell you about the next cohort and evidence how the decision was reached if you query it |
> | Applications that lead to enrolment, and the enrolment record itself | 6 years from the end of the financial year in which the programme ends | Contract, accounting and tax record-keeping |
> | A minimal completion record (name, programme, cohort dates, completion status) | Retained beyond 6 years | So we can confirm your attendance to you or a future employer on request. Everything else in the enrolment record is deleted at 6 years |
> | Access-support information you give us | Deleted as soon as the programme's final session ends | It is more sensitive than the rest of your application, so it is kept for no longer than the arrangements it exists to make |
> | Case material and session working documents | Deleted within 90 days of the 30-day implementation review | The material is confidential and has no purpose after the review |
> | Enquiry correspondence that does not become an application | 12 months from the last contact | Ordinary business correspondence |
> | Payment and invoice records | 6 years from the end of the relevant financial year | HMRC record-keeping |
>
> We delete or anonymise information at the end of these periods. Where we are
> required by law to keep something for longer, we keep only what the law requires.

## 3. Access-support and health information (Article 9 condition, and the Article 13 rights set)

> **Information about access needs**
>
> If you tell us about a disability, health condition or access requirement, that
> is "special category" information and the law gives it extra protection.
>
> We ask for it for one purpose only: to arrange the adjustments that will let you
> take part fully. We rely on **your explicit consent** to hold it (UK GDPR
> Article 9(2)(a)), and separately on our legitimate interests in running the
> programme and our duty to make reasonable adjustments under the Equality Act
> 2010 as our basis for processing it at all (Article 6(1)(f)).
>
> Giving us this information is entirely your choice. If you prefer, you can
> select "prefer to discuss privately" and speak to the principal directly
> instead of writing anything down. Declining to answer has no effect on whether
> your application succeeds, and the information is not used in the selection
> decision.
>
> It is held separately from the rest of your application, is seen only by the
> principal and by anyone strictly necessary to make an arrangement, is never
> included in analytics or reporting, and is deleted as soon as the programme's
> final session ends.
>
> You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to [PROGRAMME EMAIL], and
> we will delete the information. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything we
> did lawfully before you withdrew it, and we may then be unable to make the
> arrangement it related to.

Implementation note: the application form's access-needs step needs an explicit
consent tick — "I consent to Bright Leadership Consulting holding this
information in order to arrange adjustments" — rather than treating the answer
itself as consent. Because we rely on explicit consent, no Appropriate Policy
Document under Schedule 1 of the Data Protection Act 2018 is required; if the
basis ever changes to a Schedule 1 condition, one becomes mandatory.

## 4. Cancellation, and starting before the 14 days are up (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, regs 29–30 and 36–37)

> **Your right to cancel — individuals**
>
> If you enrol as an individual (that is, not through your employer or your own
> business), you have a legal right to cancel within **14 days** of the day we
> confirm your place, without giving a reason and without penalty. To cancel,
> tell us in writing at [PROGRAMME EMAIL]. We will refund everything you have
> paid within 14 days of receiving your cancellation.
>
> **If you ask us to start before the 14 days are up.** Because places are
> confirmed close to the start date, the programme may begin during your
> cancellation period. If you ask us to begin — by starting orientation,
> submitting case material, or attending a session — then:
>
> - if you cancel part-way through, we may charge you a proportionate amount for
>   what has already been delivered, and refund the rest; and
> - once the programme has been **fully delivered** within the 14 days at your
>   express request, you lose the right to cancel.
>
> We will ask you to confirm both of these in writing before the programme
> begins. If you do not give that confirmation, we will not start delivery until
> your 14 days have passed, which may mean deferring you to a later cohort.
>
> **Employer-funded and business places.** Where the place is bought by an
> employer, a company, a partnership or you in the course of your own business,
> the statutory 14-day cancellation right does not apply — it is a right given to
> consumers only. Those places are governed by the cancellation and transfer
> terms set out at [CLAUSE REFERENCE] instead.

Implementation note: the enrolment confirmation step needs two separate ticks,
recorded with a timestamp against the application:

1. "I expressly request that the programme begins before my 14-day cancellation
   period ends."
2. "I acknowledge that once the programme has been fully delivered at my request,
   I will lose my right to cancel."

Regulation 37 makes the acknowledgement, not just the request, the operative
condition — without it a refund can be claimed after full delivery.
