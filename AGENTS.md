Employer-funded ELM offer records share contracting identity and invoice constants from `src/data/legal.ts` so the offer, invoice, acceptance record, and Terms page stay aligned.
Mailbox routing uses `info@brightleadershipconsulting.com` for programme/pre-purchase enquiries and `admin@brightleadershipconsulting.com` for accepted purchases, invoices, payment, access, cancellations and contractual administration, so transactional records do not mix enquiry and administration contacts.
Internal enquiry notifications send from the `submit-form` edge function via the Resend connector gateway (`_shared/resendNotify.ts`); they are best-effort and must never block or fail a form submission, and mail can only leave once `brightleadershipconsulting.com` is verified in the Resend account.
Webinar captions render in a separate strip below the video so they never obscure slide content.- Enquiry attribution (contact_submissions.enquiry_type/source) is carried only in the explicit enquiry-route URL and cleared after submission, so unrelated enquiries are never tagged; both values are allowlisted in submit-form.
- Cookie consent has two independent categories (analytics → analytics_storage, advertising → ad_*) stored as a v2 record; older records are discarded so visitors are re-asked after wording changes.
- Campaign tags (utm_source/medium/campaign) are held in memory only (no storage), allow-listed client- and server-side, and malformed values are dropped rather than rejecting an enquiry.
- Webinar viewing events use one-second watched slots (normal speed, no skips/replays counted) and are sent only with analytics consent and no identifiers.
- Marketing opt-ins are stored in marketing_consents with server-held consent wording keyed by version; every marketing send must exclude any email with a withdrawn record.
